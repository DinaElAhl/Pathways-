# Roots content and services refresh — 13 October 2026

## Identity and method

The supplied social posts are visual references, not website content. Remove the class poster; retain the green, mint, yellow, coral, rounded typography, and original native sprout artwork. ## Qur’anic learning cards

All 58 existing cards have revised editorial observations, reflection questions, and concrete actions. Each has an English meaning, an Arabic explanatory gloss, links to its existing verse references, and a copyable mini-lesson. Related Names are paired on some cards; 58 cards is not an exhaustive count of divine Names.

Corrected the al-Ḥakīm reference from 6:114 to 31:27 and 59:24. Added 58:1 for the hearing-of-a-complaint example. Restored the judgement context of al-Fattāḥ in 34:26. Replaced unsupported physical comparisons, promises of a particular cure/outcome, dismissive language about grief or orphanhood, and instructions that could discourage appropriate assistance or protective boundaries.

These are pedagogical revisions, not a claim that every gloss or enumeration has received independent scholarly approval. The public text labels reflections as distinct from Qur’anic text and directs readers to context and tafsir. Primary checks included Quran.com 59:23–24, 42:11, 35:15, 34:26, 31:27, 58:1, 2:255, 39:53, 27:40, 51:58, 30:50, 57:3, and 24:35. A qualified teacher should review material for the intended age and scholarly approach before using it as a formal theology curriculum.
