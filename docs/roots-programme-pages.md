# Separate Roots programme pages

- /start is the shared entry for a single Roots Instagram account.
- /roots-national is the English landing page for the Egyptian curriculum. It links existing /join and /teach forms, with no changes to their Google receiver.
- /learn-arabic is for non-native Arabic learners. It separates elementary recordings from teen/adult placement and practice, and explains that age and proficiency differ. C/B/A are internal stages, not CEFR.
- Homepage and More menu expose both programmes. Existing National static pages and links remain available.

The non-native enquiry prepares an email only; the visitor reviews and sends it in their mail app. It is not a server-backed admission form, account, paid booking or automatic email. No learner personal information is persisted. Recordings are elementary previews; this change does not claim adult courses, comprehensive advanced coverage or paid worksheet fulfilment are active.

Validation: production build (131 prerendered routes), isolated mobile-browser checks for all three pages, cross-programme links, correct National form links and enquiry preparation without sending email.
